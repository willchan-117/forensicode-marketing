No AI detection · No content analysis · Local-only

The challenge

Generative AI has made authorship increasingly difficult to evidence using final submissions alone.

  • Text-based detectors are probabilistic and inconsistent
  • False positives raise fairness and appeal risks
  • Assessment teams need clearer, defensible signals

A different approach

ForensiCode records how work is written, not what is written.

By focusing on the writing process itself, ForensiCode provides evidence that is harder to fabricate, easier to explain, and better aligned with institutional expectations of fairness, transparency, and due process.

How it works

01

Write normally

A lightweight Word add-in records high-level writing activity locally on the student’s device.

02

Generate a report

A cryptographically linked process report summarises how the document was written over time.

03

Submit both files

The document and report are submitted together through existing institutional workflows.

04

Verify locally

If authorship questions arise, staff can verify authenticity locally, with no uploads and no external servers.
